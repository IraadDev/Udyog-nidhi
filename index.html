FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for industrial subsidies?

Eligibility depends on business location, industry type, investment amount, government scheme conditions, and project status.

Can existing MIDC units apply for subsidies?

Yes. Some existing units, expansion projects, and new investments may be eligible depending on the applicable scheme.

Do you help with documentation?

Yes. Udyognidhi Consultancy provides support for subsidy documents, project reports, declarations, compliance records, and supporting files.

Do you provide project finance support?

Yes. We support project finance advisory, term loans, working capital guidance, and financial structuring.

Do you guarantee subsidy approval?

No consultancy should promise guaranteed approval. Subsidy approval depends on eligibility, documents, government rules, and department review. We provide professional guidance and claim support.
